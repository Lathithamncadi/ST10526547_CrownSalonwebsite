Our story

Every chair tells a different story.

We started this salon on one idea: a good look shouldn't ask you to choose between hair, lashes, brows, braids, and nails — it should hold all of it. Our stylists cross-train across every specialty on this floor, so the same hands that laminate your brows can talk you through a color change or a fresh set of braids. No handing you off, no re-explaining what you want.

"We don't do one thing well. We do five things well, together."

  • Hair Styling

    Cuts, color, and blowouts built around how you actually wear your hair.

  • Lashes

    Classic to volume sets, mapped to your natural lash line.

  • Brow Lamination

    Fuller, groomed brows that hold their shape for weeks.

  • Braids

    Protective and statement styles, sized to your scalp and schedule.

  • Nails

    Manicures and sets finished with the same care as the rest of the room.
